# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design peers, collaborators, and anyone professionally curious who lands here from LinkedIn or Dribbble — including hiring managers, though the site never courts them. They skim first, judge craft instantly, and only read deeply if the first screen earns it.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Paweł Mikołajek, product designer and builder at Human Made. It publishes interesting shipped work — internal tools live in production, motion that ships, front-ends he built himself — and lets the craft speak for itself. Success = a visitor leaves convinced he designs *and* ships.

**Hard constraint:** the site must NOT openly state availability for hire ("open to roles", "available for work", etc.). Paweł works at an agency and public availability signals would be badly viewed. Interest should arrive via the work, not a banner.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, rhythmic. The site practices what the About copy preaches: clarity, restraint, and the rhythm of an interface. The best compliment is that nobody notices it's designed — it just feels obvious. Voice is first-person, concrete, and unhyped.

## Anti-references

- Corporate agency site: stocky, safe, buzzword-heavy.
- AI-generated landing page scaffolding: eyebrow labels on every section, identical card grids, hero metrics, generic SaaS grammar.
- Dribbble maximalism: decoration over content, gradients and glows, mockup-only work with nothing shipped behind it.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't claim.** Project thumbnails are live re-creations of each tool's signature interaction, not static screenshots. The site itself is the portfolio piece.
2. **Motion is product motion.** Micro-interactions behave like the real product would: physical, purposeful, no bounce for bounce's sake. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative.
3. **Restraint with one voice.** Dark, hairline-ruled, type-led. One display serif (Fraunces) for headings, mono for metadata. New elements reuse this grammar instead of inventing new ones.
4. **Everything ships.** The site is a static export that runs anywhere; features that only work in a mockup don't belong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable interactions, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives (crossfade or instant state, never "nothing happens").
